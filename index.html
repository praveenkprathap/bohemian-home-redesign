THE THREAD THAT CONNECTS IT ALL

Warm, layered.
Beautifully lived in.

Bohemian brings layered textiles, cane and earthy colour. Neo-Ethnic pairs Indian heritage with contemporary lines: teak, brass, botanical art and restrained traditional motifs. Tropical Modern adds simple teak, linen and stone with a different furniture arrangement. Soft Art Deco introduces curves, walnut, muted teal and brass, with the main living room’s TV feature interpreted as an open partition. Modern Mediterranean adds pale oak, ivory linen, clay accents and stone. Sky Blue Coastal combines soft blue with warm ivory, pale oak and small navy accents. All six build on your supplied room photographs.
